# Fall Prevention Protocol

## Risk assessment
High fall risk indicators:
- Age > 65
- History of falls
- Impaired gait or balance
- Post-operative lower extremity surgery
- Blood pressure medications (risk of orthostatic hypotension)
- Confusion or cognitive impairment
- Urinary urgency or incontinence

## Interventions
- Bed in lowest position at all times
- Call light within reach — remind patient at every interaction
- Non-slip footwear when ambulating
- Keep frequently used items within reach
- Assist with toileting every 2 hours
- Ensure adequate lighting, especially at night

## For post-hip-replacement patients
- Do not cross legs
- Do not bend hip beyond 90 degrees
- Use assistive devices (walker) as directed by physical therapy
- Sit-to-stand: pause at edge of bed for 30 seconds before standing (orthostatic hypotension precaution)

## If a fall occurs
1. Do not move patient until assessed
2. Escalate to nurse immediately
3. Check for head injury, hip/limb deformity, pain
4. Document: time, location, activity at time of fall, injuries
