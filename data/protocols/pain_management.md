# Pain Management Protocol

## Assessment
Use the Numeric Rating Scale (NRS) 0–10. Ask: "On a scale of 0 to 10, where 0 is no pain and 10 is the worst pain you can imagine, how would you rate your pain right now?"

Pain levels:
- 0–3: Mild — non-pharmacologic interventions first (repositioning, ice/heat, distraction)
- 4–6: Moderate — consider scheduled analgesics per care plan
- 7–10: Severe — escalate to nurse immediately; do not delay

## Non-pharmacologic interventions
- Repositioning every 2 hours
- Ice packs for acute swelling (20 min on, 20 min off)
- Heat packs for muscle spasm (not for acute injury or open wounds)
- Deep breathing exercises
- Distraction and relaxation techniques

## When to escalate
Escalate immediately if:
- Pain > 7/10 unrelieved by analgesics
- New onset severe pain not previously present
- Pain accompanied by shortness of breath, chest tightness, or diaphoresis
- Suspected compartment syndrome: severe pain out of proportion, paresthesia, pallor

## Post-operative pain
Expected after hip replacement: 3–6/10 in the first 72 hours. Should decrease by day 3–5.
Persistent severe pain beyond day 5 warrants surgical team review.
