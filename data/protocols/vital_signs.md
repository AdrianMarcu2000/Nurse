# Vital Signs — Normal Ranges and Escalation Thresholds

## Temperature
- Normal adult: 97.8–99.1 °F (36.5–37.3 °C)
- Low-grade fever: 99.1–100.4 °F
- Fever: > 100.4 °F (38.0 °C) — notify nurse
- High fever: > 103 °F — escalate immediately
- Hypothermia: < 96.8 °F — escalate immediately

## Blood Pressure
- Normal: 90–120 / 60–80 mmHg
- Elevated: 120–129 / < 80
- Stage 1 HTN: 130–139 / 80–89 — notify nurse if new
- Stage 2 HTN: ≥ 140 / ≥ 90 — notify nurse
- Hypertensive crisis: > 180 / > 120 — escalate immediately
- Hypotension: systolic < 90 — escalate immediately (fall risk, shock)

## Heart Rate
- Normal: 60–100 bpm
- Bradycardia: < 60 bpm — notify nurse if symptomatic
- Tachycardia: > 100 bpm — notify nurse if sustained
- Escalate immediately: < 40 or > 150 bpm

## Oxygen Saturation (SpO2)
- Normal: 95–100%
- Borderline: 92–94% — apply supplemental O2, notify nurse
- Critical: < 92% — escalate immediately

## Blood Glucose
- Fasting normal: 70–100 mg/dL
- Hypoglycemia: < 70 mg/dL — give glucose, notify nurse immediately
- Hyperglycemia: > 250 mg/dL — notify nurse
- Diabetic emergency: < 50 or > 400 mg/dL — escalate immediately

## Frequency of monitoring (standard post-op)
- First 24h: every 4 hours
- Day 2–3: every 8 hours
- Day 4+: every 12 hours or per order
